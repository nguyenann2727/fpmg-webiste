About Foundry

Where students learn to invest in private markets.

Foundry Private Markets Group is an SMU student organization for students interested in understanding private-market investing and pursuing careers in private equity, venture capital, private credit, and other alternative investments.

We give members practical exposure to how private investments are sourced, analyzed, structured, and presented, through education, case studies, investment analysis, and interaction with industry professionals.

What we study

01

Private equity

How funds buy, improve and sell companies.

02

Search funds

How an entrepreneur raises capital to acquire and run one small business.

03

Venture capital

How investors evaluate startups and price early rounds.

04

Private credit

How non-bank lenders structure loans directly to companies.

The deal lifecycle

How a private investment takes shape.

Sourced

Where opportunities come from and how they are screened.

Analyzed

Financial analysis and valuation of the business.

Structured

How capital, terms and returns are arranged.

Presented

Making the investment case to decision-makers.
